# DANH SÁCH PROMPT TẠO ẢNH MINH HỌA CHO 20 SLIDE (BẢN CHI TIẾT CAO)
*Để có ảnh đẹp nhất từ Midjourney (v6) hoặc DALL-E 3, prompt cần chứa chi tiết về ánh sáng, góc máy, chất liệu và bối cảnh. Dưới đây là 20 prompt đã được tối ưu hóa ở mức độ "Photorealistic & Cinematic".*

---

**Slide 1: Tiêu đề (Mở đầu)**
> **Prompt:** A hyper-realistic, high-tech industrial setting featuring a Universal Robots UR5e robotic arm performing a precise pick and place task. The robot is resting on a pristine metallic desk. The background is a slightly blurred, advanced smart factory with soft glowing neon blue and orange lights. Cinematic lighting, octane render, Unreal Engine 5 style, 8k resolution, photorealistic, highly detailed metal textures and glowing LEDs.

**Slide 2: Đặt vấn đề (Sự khó khăn của Pick & Place)**
> **Prompt:** A chaotic and messy industrial conveyor belt covered with scattered, randomly oriented cylindrical objects. A robotic arm is hovering above, looking overwhelmed or struggling to find a clear path. Dim, dramatic factory lighting with heavy shadows and a slightly gritty texture. The image emphasizes the complexity of industrial automation, realistic engineering environment, highly detailed, depth of field.

**Slide 3: Mục tiêu Đồ án (Kết hợp Auto và AI)**
> **Prompt:** A stunning conceptual visualization of a UR5e robotic arm divided perfectly down the middle. The left half is made of traditional mechanical gears, steel, and blueprint lines representing rigid programming. The right half is glowing with translucent digital neural network nodes, fiber optics, and AI holograms representing Artificial Intelligence. A seamless blend of classical control and modern AI. High-tech, futuristic, glowing neon colors, 8k masterpiece.

**Slide 4: Phạm vi & Môi trường (Mô phỏng PyBullet)**
> **Prompt:** A visually striking 3D physics simulation environment showing a robotic arm and a blue industrial bin. The image captures the exact moment a digital wireframe grid matrix transforms into a solid, highly realistic 3D physical rendering. Cyberpunk aesthetics, glowing green coordinate lines, representing a computer graphics physics engine. Clean, sharp lines, isometric view, hyper-detailed.

**Slide 5: Công nghệ áp dụng (Python, PyTorch, PyQt5)**
> **Prompt:** A sleek, modern developer's desk in a dark room. On the desk are multiple glowing borderless monitors displaying dense Python code and complex PyTorch machine learning loss graphs. A robotic arm is gently interacting with the keyboard. Glowing, holographic neon logos of Python and AI floating in the air. Hacker aesthetics, cinematic moody lighting, photorealistic.

**Slide 6: Kiến trúc Tổng thể**
> **Prompt:** A conceptual 3D isometric diagram of a highly complex software architecture. Four distinct glowing glass-like layers floating perfectly above each other, representing HMI, Kinematics, AI, and Physics. Beams of bright, pulsing light connect the layers, representing real-time data flow. Tech-savvy, clean UI/UX design style, dark mode background, abstract tech art, 8k resolution.

**Slide 7: Động học Thuận (FK - Denavit Hartenberg)**
> **Prompt:** A masterpiece blueprint technical drawing of a 6-axis industrial robotic arm. Vividly glowing red, green, and blue coordinate axes (X, Y, Z vectors) are precisely attached to every single joint of the robot. Intricate mathematical notations and angles are written in glowing white chalk around the joints. Engineering precision aesthetics, dark blue architectural blueprint background, ultra-crisp lines.

**Slide 8: Động học Nghịch (IK - Hybrid Solver)**
> **Prompt:** A robotic arm reaching dynamically for a floating, intensely glowing target point in mid-air. Complex mathematical equations, Jacobian matrices, and optimization curves are floating holographically around the robot's joints. Concept of solving inverse kinematics in real-time. High-tech engineering, glowing ambient light, deep black background with bright cyan accents.

**Slide 9: Quy hoạch Quỹ đạo (Trajectory Planning)**
> **Prompt:** A beautiful, smooth, glowing neon trajectory line drawn in 3D space, showing a perfect curve from point A to point B. A robotic arm's end-effector is exactly following this curved path with a slight motion blur emphasizing smooth acceleration. In the background, a glowing trapezoidal velocity profile graph is projected on the wall. Sleek, fast motion, cinematic lighting.

**Slide 10: Chế độ Auto (Lập trình FSM cứng nhắc)**
> **Prompt:** A rigid, highly structured flowchart made of glowing solid neon signs floating inside a massive industrial factory. A robotic arm is moving mechanically and stiffly, strictly following the 90-degree straight lines of the flowchart. Concept of structured, rule-based finite state machine. Cold blue lighting, sterile industrial environment, precise and unyielding.

**Slide 11: Ứng dụng AI vào Điều hướng**
> **Prompt:** A beautiful, sleek robotic arm delicately holding a glowing, pulsating artificial brain (a complex neural network) in its end effector. Abstract data streams, binary codes, and mathematical weights flowing organically around it. Concept of deep reinforcement learning empowering mechatronics to make fluid, intelligent decisions. Futuristic, warm orange and cyan color grading.

**Slide 12: Cơ chế Hybrid Gripper & Physics Clamp**
> **Prompt:** Extreme, highly detailed macro close-up of a robotic vacuum gripper hovering just millimeters above a metallic cylindrical object. Glowing red proximity laser sensors shooting downwards from the gripper. Above the wrist joint, a holographic glowing "Lock" icon symbolizes a physics clamp keeping the wrist perfectly vertical at 90 degrees. Ultra-realistic, depth of field, mechanical details.

**Slide 13: Observation & Action Space (Vector 20D)**
> **Prompt:** A robotic arm in motion with multiple colorful, glowing 3D vector arrows (representing XYZ coordinates and Roll-Pitch-Yaw angles) radiating outwards from its wrist and base. Complex holographic HUD (Heads-Up Display) interfaces floating around the arm, displaying real-time numerical data and telemetry. Iron Man suit UI style, high-tech, highly detailed screen elements.

**Slide 14: Thiết kế Hàm Reward**
> **Prompt:** A stylized, gamified industrial interface overlaid on a robotic arm placing an object into a blue bin. Huge, glowing, floating green text saying "+500 SCORE" and "+50" popping up in mid-air, similar to a high-end video game. Gamification of industrial tasks. Bright, rewarding lighting, celebratory atmosphere, photorealistic background.

**Slide 15: Quá trình Training (Học Tăng Cường)**
> **Prompt:** A massive digital matrix displaying a grid of 16 identical robotic arms, all simultaneously attempting to pick up objects in parallel simulated environments. Some robots are failing and glowing red, while others are succeeding and glowing green. Intense data streams connecting them to a massive central AI core glowing in the ceiling. Parallel training concept, sci-fi server room aesthetic.

**Slide 16: Kết quả Training (Thành công 100%)**
> **Prompt:** A perfectly clean, brightly lit, state-of-the-art industrial setup. A sleek robotic arm is perfectly dropping a shiny metallic cylinder exactly in the geometric center of a blue bin. A massive, holographic "100% SUCCESS RATE" badge glowing triumphantly in the foreground. Professional, polished, commercial photography lighting.

**Slide 17: So sánh FSM (Auto) vs SAC (AI)**
> **Prompt:** A dramatic split-screen image. Left side: A robotic arm moving in rigid, sharp, straight robotic angles surrounded by cold blue grid lines. Right side: A robotic arm moving in a beautiful, fluid, organic, continuous curved path surrounded by warm, glowing orange energy trails. Concept of rigid logic versus flexible continuous AI action.

**Slide 18: Demo Trực tiếp phần mềm**
> **Prompt:** A close-up of a modern, sleek dark-mode desktop software interface (HMI) for controlling an industrial robot. The highly detailed screen shows 3D simulation views, complex control sliders, real-time graphs, and glowing status buttons. Professional industrial control panel aesthetics. The monitor is in a dark room with a real robot arm visible in the blurred background.

**Slide 19: Hạn chế & Hướng phát triển**
> **Prompt:** A robotic arm looking curiously directly into the lens of a highly detailed, realistic Intel RealSense depth camera. The background shows a magical transition: the left side is a virtual simulation matrix grid, and the right side is a real-world messy factory with actual lighting and dust. Concept of Sim-to-Real transfer, highly detailed camera lens reflection.

**Slide 20: Lời Cảm ơn**
> **Prompt:** A friendly, non-threatening UR5e robotic arm politely bowing down, using its gripper to delicately hand a beautiful, glowing digital origami flower to the viewer. Warm, inviting, and cinematic lighting, representing the successful and respectful conclusion of a prestigious academic defense. Soft bokeh background.
