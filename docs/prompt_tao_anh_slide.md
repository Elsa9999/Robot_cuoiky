# DANH SÁCH PROMPT TẠO ẢNH MINH HỌA CHO 20 SLIDE
*Bạn có thể dùng các prompt tiếng Anh dưới đây ném vào Midjourney, DALL-E 3 (ChatGPT), hoặc Canva/Adobe Firefly để tạo ra 20 ảnh minh họa cực chất lượng, mỗi ảnh dán vào 1 slide tương ứng.*

---

**Slide 1: Tiêu đề (Mở đầu)**
> **Prompt:** A hyper-realistic, high-tech industrial setting featuring a Universal Robots UR5e robotic arm on a clean metallic desk. Glowing blue accents, cinematic lighting, modern mechatronics concept, 8k resolution, photorealistic.

**Slide 2: Đặt vấn đề (Sự khó khăn của Pick & Place)**
> **Prompt:** A chaotic and messy industrial conveyor belt with scattered random objects. A robotic arm looking overwhelmed. Dim lighting, dramatic shadows, emphasizing the complexity of industrial automation, realistic style.

**Slide 3: Mục tiêu Đồ án (Kết hợp Auto và AI)**
> **Prompt:** A futuristic UR5e robotic arm where one half of the arm is made of traditional mechanical gears and blueprints, and the other half is glowing with digital neural network nodes and AI holograms. Concept of merging classical control with Artificial Intelligence, high-tech.

**Slide 4: Phạm vi & Môi trường (Mô phỏng PyBullet)**
> **Prompt:** A 3D physics simulation environment showing a robotic arm and a blue bin. The image transitions from a wireframe grid matrix into a solid realistic rendering. Cyberpunk aesthetics, computer graphics simulation concept.

**Slide 5: Công nghệ áp dụng (Python, PyTorch, PyQt5)**
> **Prompt:** A sleek computer monitor displaying dense Python code and PyTorch machine learning graphs. A robotic arm is interacting with the screen. Glowing neon logos of Python and AI floating in the air, modern hacker/developer desk setup.

**Slide 6: Kiến trúc Tổng thể**
> **Prompt:** A conceptual 3D isometric diagram of a software architecture. Four distinct glowing layers floating above each other, with beams of light connecting them representing data flow. Tech-savvy, clean UI/UX design style, dark mode background.

**Slide 7: Động học Thuận (FK - Denavit Hartenberg)**
> **Prompt:** A blueprint technical drawing of a 6-axis robotic arm. Glowing red, green, and blue coordinate axes (X, Y, Z) are attached to every joint of the robot. Mathematical precision, engineering aesthetics, dark blue blueprint background.

**Slide 8: Động học Nghịch (IK - Hybrid Solver)**
> **Prompt:** A robotic arm reaching for a floating glowing target point. Complex mathematical equations and matrices are floating in the air around the robot's joints. Concept of solving inverse kinematics, high-tech engineering, glowing ambient light.

**Slide 9: Quy hoạch Quỹ đạo (Trajectory Planning)**
> **Prompt:** A beautiful, smooth, glowing neon trajectory line drawn in 3D space. A robotic arm's end-effector is exactly following this curved path. In the background, a glowing trapezoidal velocity graph. Sleek, motion blur effect.

**Slide 10: Chế độ Auto (Lập trình FSM cứng nhắc)**
> **Prompt:** A rigid, blocky flowchart made of glowing neon signs floating in an industrial factory. A robotic arm is moving mechanically strictly following the straight lines of the flowchart. Concept of structured, rule-based state machine.

**Slide 11: Ứng dụng AI vào Điều hướng**
> **Prompt:** A robotic arm holding a glowing, pulsating artificial brain (neural network) in its end effector. Abstract data streams and binary codes flowing around it. Concept of deep reinforcement learning empowering mechatronics.

**Slide 12: Cơ chế Hybrid Gripper & Physics Clamp**
> **Prompt:** Extreme close-up of a robotic vacuum gripper hovering just above a cylindrical object. Glowing proximity laser sensors shooting downwards. A holographic "Lock" icon symbolizing a physics clamp keeping the wrist perfectly vertical 90 degrees.

**Slide 13: Observation & Action Space (Vector 20D)**
> **Prompt:** A robotic arm with multiple colorful 3D vector arrows (representing XYZ coordinates and Roll-Pitch-Yaw angles) radiating outwards from its wrist. Holographic HUD interfaces displaying real-time numerical data. Iron Man HUD style.

**Slide 14: Thiết kế Hàm Reward**
> **Prompt:** A stylized game-like interface overlaid on a robotic arm placing an object into a bin. Huge floating green text saying "+500 SCORE" and "+50" popping up like in a video game. Gamification of industrial tasks.

**Slide 15: Quá trình Training (Học Tăng Cường)**
> **Prompt:** A matrix of 16 identical robotic arms in a grid layout, all simultaneously attempting to pick up objects. Some are failing, some are succeeding. Glowing data streams connecting them to a central AI core. Parallel training concept.

**Slide 16: Kết quả Training (Thành công 100%)**
> **Prompt:** A perfectly clean, brightly lit industrial setup. A robotic arm perfectly dropping a shiny cylinder exactly in the center of a blue bin. A holographic "100% SUCCESS" badge glowing triumphantly in the foreground.

**Slide 17: So sánh FSM (Auto) vs SAC (AI)**
> **Prompt:** A split-screen image. Left side: A robotic arm moving in rigid, straight, robotic angles (blue theme). Right side: A robotic arm moving in a beautiful, fluid, organic curved path (orange theme). Concept of rigid logic vs flexible AI.

**Slide 18: Demo Trực tiếp phần mềm**
> **Prompt:** A modern, sleek dark-mode desktop software interface (HMI) for controlling a robot. The screen shows 3D simulation views, sliders, buttons, and real-time graphs. Professional industrial control panel aesthetics.

**Slide 19: Hạn chế & Hướng phát triển**
> **Prompt:** A robotic arm looking into a highly detailed, realistic Intel RealSense depth camera. The background shows a transition from a virtual simulation matrix into a real-world messy factory. Concept of Sim-to-Real transfer.

**Slide 20: Lời Cảm ơn**
> **Prompt:** A friendly looking UR5e robotic arm politely bowing down, handing a beautiful glowing digital flower to the viewer. Warm, inviting lighting, representing the successful conclusion of an academic defense.
